LCE comprehensive privacy notice

Your information deserves clear and responsible handling. Learn how we protect your data and how you can exercise your rights.

Approved Spanish version: 2026-11-13-lce-diagnostico-v1 · Effective since: November 13, 2026

Reference translation of the approved Spanish notice. It has not been separately approved. The original Spanish text remains authoritative. Read the approved Spanish notice.

1. Identity and address of the data controller

Under Mexico's Federal Law on Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties, Rosa Angélica Sánchez Osnaya, who provides services under the trade name LCE, Líderes en Comercio Exterior, is responsible for processing personal data. Her address is Plaza Melchor Ocampo 30, interior 5, colonia Cuauhtémoc, C.P. 06500, Ciudad de México, México.

For privacy inquiries and requests concerning your personal data: asanchez@lce.com.mx. You may also visit the address indicated.

2. Data we may collect

Depending on the contact or service, we may process data provided directly by email, telephone, in person or through our forms:

  • First and last names, email address and optional telephone number.
  • Commercial and operational information you choose to share when requesting procurement and shipment planning, logistics coordination or Mexican Official Standards support.
  • Commercial assessment answers, operation category or stage, priorities indicated and calculated result; these answers are transmitted to LCE only when you expressly choose to submit a follow-up request.
  • Submission date and time, version of the accepted notice and record of contact authorization.
  • Technical digital acquisition data, such as page visited, referring URL and campaign parameters, when available.
  • When you authorize optional analytics, interaction and browsing data collected through Microsoft Clarity, which may include pseudonymous identifiers and technical device and browser characteristics.

The questionnaire can be answered without identifying yourself and initially calculates results in the browser. We do not request sensitive personal data, passwords, electronic signatures (e.firma), confidential customs records or third-party personal data through this assessment. Avoid entering them in open fields.

3. Purposes of processing

Primary purposes: respond to inquiries; evaluate commercial requests; design and execute proposals and projects; plan procurement and shipments of international products; coordinate logistics; provide support and compliance services for Mexican Official Standards; follow up on requests sent through the assessment; and fulfill obligations arising from contractual relationships or applicable legal provisions.

Secondary, optional purpose: obtain usage statistics, observe navigation patterns and identify user experience difficulties through Microsoft Clarity when the visitor selects “Allow analytics”. Refusal does not prevent viewing services, answering the assessment or requesting contact.

Submitting an assessment request does not constitute consent to receive newsletters, mass advertising or promotional campaigns. If such communications are offered in the future, the appropriate additional authorization will be requested.

4. Cookies, similar technologies and Microsoft Clarity

This site may use essential technical storage to remember certain browser preferences. It also integrates Microsoft Clarity (project ID zd6brtj9wu) for optional analytics. Clarity makes it possible to study events such as page navigation, clicks, scrolling and session representations, and to obtain technical browser or device characteristics.

After receiving affirmative analytics consent, Microsoft Clarity may set first-party cookies such as _clck (pseudonymous association of visits and preferences) and _clsk (grouping pages in the same session). The service may also use cookies from Microsoft domains, including CLID, MUID, ANONCHK, MR or SM, according to its terms and configuration. Not all of them will necessarily be set on every visit.

Clarity is not downloaded or started by this integration before you authorize analytics. You may choose “Reject” or “Allow analytics” in the initial notice and view or change that decision through “Analytics preferences” on the site. Revocation prevents future collection through the integration after applying the change and reloading the page; if previous cookies persist, you can also remove them through your browser settings.

To reduce exposure of personal information in session representations, the form and assessment fields are marked for masking. Clarity is used here to improve the site experience, not to deliver individualized advertising from LCE. Technical information may be processed by Microsoft in accordance with the service terms and the Microsoft privacy statement. See also the technical description of Clarity cookies.

5. Processing, providers and transfers

Data submitted through the assessment form is stored in Azure Table Storage with access controls to LCE's tables through managed identity. To operate the services, technology providers for infrastructure and analytics, including Microsoft, may process information on LCE's behalf under relevant agreements and conditions; depending on the service, part of the technical processing may take place outside Mexico.

LCE does not sell form data or provide it to third parties for their own campaigns. If an operation entails additional transfers to third parties requiring consent under law, consent will be requested when necessary. Data may be communicated to competent authorities where an applicable legal obligation exists.

6. Security and retention

LCE applies administrative and technical measures proportionate to the nature of the information to restrict unauthorized access and protect its confidentiality and integrity. Information will be retained as long as necessary to handle requests, fulfill legal or contractual obligations and manage claims; it will then be deleted or blocked when legally appropriate. The organization will review its internal retention periods according to the type of record.

7. ARCO rights, restriction and revocation

You may request access, rectification, cancellation or objection regarding your data, as well as restrict certain uses or revoke consent where legally appropriate, by writing to asanchez@lce.com.mx or submitting a written request at the controller's address.

Your request must state your name, means of receiving a response, the data or rights concerned and, where applicable, reasonable documentation proving identity or representation. To rectify information, describe the changes and attach supporting evidence. LCE will communicate its determination within the statutory twenty-day period and, where appropriate, implement it within the following fifteen days; these periods may be extended in the cases provided by law.

Rejecting or withdrawing analytics consent is independent of requesting commercial assistance. You can adjust this option in “Analytics preferences” without interrupting use of the assessment.

8. Notice changes and competent authority

Changes to this notice will be made available through this site and the page lce.com.mx/index.php/privacy-policy-es/, indicating the version and update date. Where legally required, LCE will communicate substantial changes through available contact channels.

If you believe your personal data protection rights have been violated, you may contact the Secretaría Anticorrupción y Buen Gobierno, under applicable Mexican federal legislation.
