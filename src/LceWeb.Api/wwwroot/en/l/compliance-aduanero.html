02 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before deciding.

What does preventive compliance mean?

Preparing information and reviewing requirements before purchasing or shipping decisions, rather than addressing them only when an incident arises.

Does a complete file guarantee customs clearance?

No. Eligibility and compliance depend on applicable rules and the specific case; they must be reviewed with authorized parties.

Does LCE provide tax defense or certify the product?

This page describes coordination, planning and requirements management. It does not offer tax defense or replace an authority, customs broker or certification body.
