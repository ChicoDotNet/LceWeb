02 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before deciding.

Does LCE certify that a supplier is safe?

We do not guarantee the absence of fraud or provide a solvency opinion. The scope of a supplier review is agreed case by case with the relevant specialists.

Can I consult before making a purchase?

Yes. Procurement and shipment planning makes it possible to assess alternatives before committing resources.

What information is useful to have?

Product, country, quantities, deadline, purchase terms and transport needs.
