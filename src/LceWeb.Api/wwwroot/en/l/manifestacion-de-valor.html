02 / FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before deciding.

When does the new schedule apply?

The Third Resolution amending the 2026 General Foreign Trade Rules (RGCE), published on November 23, 2026, sets staggered dates: January 1, 2027 for temporary imports and January 15 for definitive imports, for the applicable operations. Consult the Eleventh transitional provision and its conditions; exceptions or subsequent changes may apply.

Does LCE replace the party responsible for transmission?

No. We review the operational context and coordinate information preparation with the relevant participants and specialists. Scope is confirmed for each case.

What can I prepare before the call?

Product, origin, planned customs regime, stage, purchase terms and list of available documents. Do not submit credentials or sensitive tax information in the public form.

Primary source: DOF, November 23, 2026, Third Resolution amending the 2026 RGCE. Reviewed on November 25, 2026.
